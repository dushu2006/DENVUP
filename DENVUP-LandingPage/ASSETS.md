## Service plates (`public/assets/plates/`)

Five deterministic vector plates, generated by `node scripts/build-plates.mjs` — not stock, not a template, not client work. Each is 1200 × 900 on the paper ground `#f6f1e8` with ink `#0b0a09`, an oxide rule and a 75 px hairline grid; the geometry is derived from the service name and a fixed seed, so a rebuild reproduces the identical file.

| File | Size | Service | Geometry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `web-design.svg` | 1.7 kB | Web design & development | Column measure, baseline rules, one oxide block |
| `ai-solutions.svg` | 3.0 kB | AI-powered solutions | Node field with weighted edges |
| `integrations.svg` | 6.1 kB | Business integrations | Connector bus with junctions |
| `interactive.svg` | 2.0 kB | Creative & interactive | Nested arcs and a rotating tick ring |
| `automation.svg` | 1.8 kB | Automation & business tools | Sequential gates and a return path |

Used in three places: `.svc-card__plate` on `/services`, `figure.svc-hero__art > img` on each service detail (with the caption “Plate drawn for this page. Not client work.”), and `.svc-arts > img.svc-art` in the home selector. They are illustrations of the service, never presented as screenshots or client work.

**Swapping them.** Replace a file with a photograph or a commissioned illustration of the same aspect ratio and update its `alt`; the layout uses `object-fit: cover` inside a 4:3 frame, so no other change is required. Until then these plates are the final artwork, and the owner-to-confirm list records that no photography exists.

## Generated artwork that is code, not files

The following are produced at runtime and therefore have no binary asset:

- the opening box, colour field and wipe (CSS variables + clip-path)
- hero orbit particles, constellation, flow field, dust and ripples (Canvas 2D)
- the 3D depth scene, tilt cards, isometric plate and carousel (CSS 3D)
- the SVG atlas, line art, diagrams, arrows and rings (inline SVG)
- grain, glow, scanlines and gradients (CSS)

This keeps the network payload small and makes every visual inspectable in source. The trade-off is recorded in `IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md`.

## Alt text and fallbacks

- Every `<img>` has an `alt`; decorative images use `alt=""`.
- Canvas demos render a static poster or explanatory paragraph when a 2D context is unavailable.
- Videos and audio are not used; there is no missing-media reference anywhere in the build.
