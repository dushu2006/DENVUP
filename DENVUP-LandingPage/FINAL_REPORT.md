## 1. What was built

**Fifteen routes, each a real prerendered HTML file:** `/`, `/services`, five service details (`web-design`, `ai-solutions`, `integrations`, `interactive`, `automation`), `/lab`, `/concepts`, `/studio`, `/pricing`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms` and a designed `/404`. Navigation between them is soft, progressive enhancement: the next document is fetched, only `<main>` is swapped inside `document.startViewTransition`, history and title update, focus moves to the new `h1`, and the header, stage and cursor stay mounted. Without the API, or on any failure, it is a normal page load. If the transition has not started in 220 ms the swap is applied directly.

**Home — sixteen chapters (0–15):** first-visit intro (skippable, tied to real loading, ≤4 s) → white prelude whose bottom-centre box floods the screen orange → hero inside that colour with the one signature plate → ink manifesto with scroll-linked highlighting → services selector with a pointer-following band → pinned service deep dives → capabilities with a live stage → type/scroll/3D demos naming technique IDs → process timeline → technology atlas (confirmed only) → studio (typographic, no invented faces) → concepts (labelled) → pricing (₹15,000 / ₹20,000 / ₹50,000, selector, estimator, third-party note) → FAQ → multi-step contact → closing and dense footer.

**Global systems:** a persistent fixed stage whose colour, camera and shapes change per chapter; motion tokens that declare trigger, duration, easing, reverse, mobile and reduced-motion behaviour; a compressing header; a full-screen curtain menu and mobile sheet; journey navigator; keyboard guide; a pointer-only dot + ring + contextual labels + trailing echo whose ink is chosen from the ground under the pointer; magnetic buttons; microinteractions; reading progress; audio off by default; native scrolling; static and no-canvas fallbacks; reduced motion that removes travel but keeps every section and every word.

**Content and artwork:** all copy written for this build; five deterministic SVG service plates generated by `scripts/build-plates.mjs` (paper/ink/oxide, one geometry per service) used on `/services`, each service detail and the home selector; supplied logos used unmodified in header, intro, footer, favicon, touch icon and social card.

## 3. Failures found, and what happened to them

1. **The cursor vanished on coloured grounds.** Measured at **1.02–1.15:1** over white forms, dark cards and demo canvases — the ring used the chapter's ink, not the ground's. Fixed in `src/client/cursor.ts`: the probe reads the painted ground at the pointer, composites image pixels and demo panels, falls back to the scene plus a two-tone rim, and re-reads on pointermove, scroll and `scrollend`. Retested at 104 points: minimum 5.87:1.
2. **Soft navigation could stall for ~1.1 s and leave `is-routing` stuck** after a cancelled click. A no-op View Transition was measured at 1132 ms. Fixed with a 220 ms watchdog and a counter cleared in `finally`; navigation now measures 641 ms and Back works.
3. **`/404` failed axe `heading-order`.** Added a visually hidden `h2`; axe is clean.
4. **The build dirtied tracked files.** `npm run build` ran the dev prerender into the project root. The generated route folders are no longer tracked (or rebuilt by `build`), and are ignored; `git status` is clean after a build.
5. **Two test artefacts masqueraded as defects.** The cursor sweep sampled mid–smooth-scroll (scrollY 16 of 14,850) and the skip-link test sampled at 500 ms. The tests now wait for the scroll to settle; the product behaviour was correct.
