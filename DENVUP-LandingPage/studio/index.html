Manifesto

A website is not a picture of a business.
It is the business, at the moment somebody
is deciding whether to trust you.

That is the whole argument for doing this carefully. If the page is slow, the message is that you are careless. If the words are vague, the message is that you have not decided what you want. If the motion stutters on a phone, the message is that you built it for a demo instead of for a customer.

So we build the argument, not the decoration. Every choice on a page — the order of the sections, the size of the type, the moment the picture arrives, the way the form behaves when it fails — is part of the message. And all of it has to survive the worst conditions: a small screen, a slow connection, a keyboard, a screen reader, a visitor who has already decided they do not care.

This site is the argument. Every technique on it is real, every transition has a rule behind it, and every effect you can trigger here is one we would be willing to build for you. We would rather show the machinery than describe it.

Six rules the work is built on

These are not aspirations. They are the checks we run before a site is called finished, and the reason some briefs are declined.

  1. 01

    The browser is the medium

    We show working pages, not flat pictures. Layout, motion and type only make sense in the thing they will live in, so the design is reviewed in the browser from the first week.

  2. 02

    Motion has to be a system

    One signature moment per page, and everything else has to earn its place. Rules get written down, so the next developer can extend the motion without breaking it.

  3. 03

    The content decides the layout

    We design around what you actually have. If the content is thin, the page is honest about being thin rather than padded with placeholders.

  4. 04

    It has to work for everyone

    Keyboard, touch, reduced motion, small screens, slow connections. Those are not extra features at the end; they are the definition of done.

  5. 05

    Say the difficult thing early

    The number, the limitation, the date it will not be ready. A visitor who finds the catch themselves stops trusting everything else, including the parts that were true.

  6. 06

    Hand it over properly

    The code, the decisions and the reasons for them, in writing. A site that only its author can maintain is a site that will slowly rot.
