Business integrations

Your tools, wired to each other and to the site.

Start a conversation Indicative prices

We connect what you already run: CRMs, payment gateways, inventory, scheduling, email, analytics, ERP. The work is mostly unglamorous and mostly decisive — getting the data to land in the right place, in the right shape, without anybody maintaining it by hand.

Every integration is drawn as a line between two named systems before a line of code is written.

Who it is for
Businesses whose website sits apart from the tools that actually run the business.
What it produces
One connected flow: a form becomes a record, a payment becomes an order, a booking becomes a calendar entry.

The 6 stages it usually moves through

The same process runs behind every line. What changes is the work inside each stage, and how long it takes.

  1. 01 Discovery We find out what the site is actually for
  2. 02 Planning The order of the work, written down
  3. 03 Design A direction you can react to, not a deck
  4. 04 Development The build is the design, not a copy of it
  5. 05 Testing Checked on the devices people actually use
  6. 06 Launch & handover Ship it, then stay reachable

The full process, with everything that comes out of each stage, is on the home page.

What if one of the systems has no usable API?

Then we say so in the first conversation, because it changes the price and the timeline. There is usually a workable route — a scheduled export, a shared database table, a webhook from the other side — but it is never as clean as a documented API, and pretending otherwise is how projects slip.
