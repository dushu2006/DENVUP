What is in every quote

Discovery

A written brief, a sitemap, a technical recommendation and a fixed scope. If we cannot scope it, we will say so rather than guess.

Design

A direction with the reasoning behind it, and working responsive pages in a browser — not a deck of flat images.

Build

Semantic markup, real fonts, images sized for their slot, and animation that runs on transform and opacity.

Motion

The interaction layer, the reduced-motion pass, and the technique library that comes with the handover.

Launch

The launch checklist, analytics and search basics, and documentation somebody on your side can actually use.

Care

We stay reachable afterwards for changes, iterations and the next thing. A maintenance option is available if you want one.
