Things people ask before the first message

What does a project usually cost?

Landing pages start from ₹15,000, business websites from ₹20,000 and advanced custom solutions from ₹50,000. Those are indicative starting points, they move with scope, and a written quote replaces them.

See the pricing page for what moves the number.

How long does it take?

A landing page is usually two to three weeks. A business website runs five to seven. Advanced work with integrations or AI in it is usually eight to twelve, and we will say so if that is too long for your deadline.

Do you work with existing content?

Yes, and it is usually the fastest route. We will tell you what we can keep, what needs rewriting, and what is missing. Rewriting is part of the work rather than a charge on top of it.

Can you work with our developer?

Yes. We are happy to design against an existing stack, to write the front end against your API, or to hand over a design system and documentation for your own team to build from.

What if we are not sure what we need?

That is the normal place to start, and it is what the first conversation is for. Describe the problem in a sentence and we will tell you which of the five service lines it belongs to — and if it belongs to none of them, we will say that too.
