Accessibility statement

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline: semantic landmarks, one main heading per page, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, labelled fields, and text contrast checked by hand.

Motion is optional. A full reduced-motion path keeps every section and all content, removes pinning, parallax, cursor effects and auto-playing movement, and stops decorative canvases.

The contact form has no server endpoint in this build, so it cannot store or transmit a message. It says so, and offers a prefilled email and WhatsApp instead. No cookies are set and no third-party analytics load.

If something does not work, email denvupforyou@gmail.com with the page and what happened.

Privacy, in plain language

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and assets are served from this site.

The contact form transmits nothing: there is no endpoint. Email and WhatsApp links open your own application, and the message is sent by you, through that provider.

Your details are used to answer the enquiry and for nothing else. Ask us to delete the conversation and we will.
