Privacy

We do not collect
anything.

This is the whole policy, and it is short because the site is simple. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts, no accounts and no server. The contact form does not send anything to us either.

The policy in full

What this site stores

Nothing on your device, except one thing: your operating system's “reduce motion” preference is honoured, and the site remembers whether you have already seen the opening sequence in this browser session. That value is kept in your browser's own session storage and is discarded when the session ends. We cannot read it and neither can anyone else.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies, and therefore shows no cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.

Analytics

None. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no pixel, and no heat-map service. We do not know how many people read this page, and we would rather say that than collect it.

Third parties

None. Every font, script, style and image on this site is served from this domain. There is no CDN, no embedded video player, no map, no chat widget and no social media frame. The only outbound links are the email address and the two WhatsApp numbers, which you have to choose to click.

The contact form

The form on this site has no server behind it. When you complete it, your browser builds the message and opens your own mail application with it filled in. Nothing is transmitted by this page, nothing is stored by us, and the site never says that a message has been sent — because it has not been sent by the site. It is sent by you, from your mail application, when you choose to send it.

Your mail application and WhatsApp

Once you leave this site, your email provider and WhatsApp apply their own privacy policies. We have no access to them, and we do not ask them for information about you.

Children

This site is a business site and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this site ever adds analytics, a form endpoint or a third-party embed, this page changes first, and the change is written down. If a future version of the site collects anything, this paragraph will not still say it collects nothing.

A question about this

Ask us and we will answer plainly, including if the answer is that we do not know.
