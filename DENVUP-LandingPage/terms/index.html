Legal

Terms

The working terms for an engagement. Short, and written to be read before a contract, not instead of one.

Quotations

Published prices are starting points, not quotations. A written estimate follows a scope conversation and lists what is included, what is excluded, and every third-party cost. Work starts when that estimate is accepted.

Scope and changes

A change to the agreed scope is quoted before it is built. Where a change invalidates work already done, that work is billed. We will always say this before starting, not afterwards.

Payment

A deposit before work starts and the balance on delivery, unless the written estimate says otherwise. Third-party subscriptions, domains and model usage are paid by the client, in their own accounts, so they always keep control.

Ownership

On final payment, the client owns the delivered design and code for their project. We keep the right to describe the work and to show it in a portfolio unless the estimate says otherwise. The DENVUP mark and the studio’s own pre-existing code remain ours.

What we do not promise

No delivery date is promised on this website. No ranking, traffic or revenue outcome is promised. Where a dependency is outside our control — a third-party API, a hosting provider, a model provider — the estimate names it.

Owner to confirm

Governing law and jurisdiction; the deposit percentage; the warranty period after delivery; the invoicing entity and tax details. These are flagged in ASSETS.md for the owners, not assumed here.

Last updated 19 October 2026. Items marked “owner to confirm” are listed in ASSETS.md and are not presented as settled.

Accessibility statement

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline: semantic landmarks, one main heading per page, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, labelled fields, and text contrast checked by hand.

Motion is optional. A full reduced-motion path keeps every section and all content, removes pinning, parallax, cursor effects and auto-playing movement, and stops decorative canvases.

The contact form has no server endpoint in this build, so it cannot store or transmit a message. It says so, and offers a prefilled email and WhatsApp instead. No cookies are set and no third-party analytics load.

If something does not work, email denvupforyou@gmail.com with the page and what happened.

Privacy, in plain language

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and assets are served from this site.

The contact form transmits nothing: there is no endpoint. Email and WhatsApp links open your own application, and the message is sent by you, through that provider.

Your details are used to answer the enquiry and for nothing else. Ask us to delete the conversation and we will.
